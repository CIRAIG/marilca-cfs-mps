# Changelog

All notable changes to the MarILCA PEBBLE characterization factors are listed here.
Each release is archived on Zenodo; all versions: [10.5281/zenodo.20087936](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20087936).

## v1.1.0 - 2026-10-28

### Changes in the characterization factors
- **Tire and road wear particles (TRWP):** TRWP-specific effect factors (physical effects) in water and soil (Ospital et al., 2026), in `02_Run_SB_CI.R` and `04_Run_SB_Dyn.R`. The sediment effect factor is unchanged.
  - EEF water: 18.521 [1.688 - 203.244] PAF·m³/kg (95% CI)
  - EEF soil: 0.197 [0.038 - 2.141] PAF·m³/kg (95% CI)
  - Effect on the CFs: most TRWP CFs change by less than 10%, because TRWP mostly ends up in sediments. Marine CFs decrease where TRWP remains in the water column (down to 0.1x for emissions to seawater surface). Terrestrial CFs increase by about 1.1-1.2x, with a wider uncertainty range.
- **Linen:** updated degradation data in soil (`input/SI_B.xlsx`), based on Alliance for European Flax-Linen and Hemp (2025). Linen CFs decrease for emissions reaching soil (down to about 0.4x).
- **SimpleBox (SBoo):** updated to version 2026.4.0 (no noticeable effect on the CFs).
- **TRWP redistribution:** updated pre-fate redistribution data (`input/Redistribution_prefate_TRWPs.xlsx`).
- CFs of the other materials change by less than 5% (Monte Carlo variability).
- Results regenerated: `results/SI_C.xlsx` (steady state) and `results/SI_D.xlsx` (time horizons).
- See `figures/Comparison_with_previous_version_CF_ratios.png` for the change of the CFs per material compared to v1.0.0.

### New
- **ReCiPe CFs** (`results/ReCiPe/ReCiPe_CF.xlsx`, generated by `results/ReCiPe/ReCiPe_CF.R`) for the three cultural perspectives (Individualist: 20 years, Hierarchist: 100 years, Egalitarian: steady state):
  - Midpoint CFs in kg PET, 1µm, sphere-eq (emitted to river water, seawater surface or natural soil, World region)
  - Endpoint CFs in species·yr/kg
  - Midpoint to endpoint conversion factors
- **Comparison with a previous version:** `Compare_with_main.R` (ratios of all CFs in SI_C and SI_D vs another git branch) and `figures/Plot_comparison_with_main.R` (figure per material).

### Code and performance
- `02_Run_SB_CI.R` is about 6x faster (full run of about 1.6 hours on 7 cores):
  - The calculation of CFs from FFs is vectorized over all Monte Carlo runs and emission compartments.
  - SBoo only builds the k matrices (the unused SBoo steady-state solution is skipped).
  - Steady-state and time-horizon FFs share the same matrix inversion.
  - Polymers are run in parallel on macOS/Linux (`n_cores`); `n_cores = 1` runs serially.
- `03_Default_CF.R` creates SI_C and SI_D in one run.
- `InstallSBoo.R` installs the SBoo 2026.4.0 release.
- Figures 2 and 5 updated.
- Repository cleanup: the graphing code (`SI_Graphing.ipynb`, `Plot_comparison_with_main.R`) is moved to `figures/`, the obsolete `Compare_outcomes.R` (replaced by `Compare_with_main.R`) and Jupyter checkpoint files are removed, and `.gitignore` is simplified.
